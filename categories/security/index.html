Fundamentals of Differential Privacy: From Definitions to ML Applications

July 8, 2025 Introduction With the proliferation of intelligent devices, data volume has grown explosively, providing rich training material for machine learning. However, training datasets often contain sensitive information and therefore pose serious privacy risks. For example, membership inference attacks can infer whether an individual’s data was used to train a model. In IoT settings, federated learning keeps raw data local, but research shows that exchanged model updates (gradients) can still leak information. Traditional cryptographic methods are often too computationally heavy for realtime training.
