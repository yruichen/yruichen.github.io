About#

I am an undergraduate student at Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT). My research interests lie in embodied AI applications and AI for Science, with a current focus on:

  • 3D Scene Reconstruction (3DGS / 4DGS)
  • Computational Pathology & Spatial Transcriptomics: medical image segmentation, cross-modal prediction, and network architecture optimization.

I welcome research discussions and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out if our interests overlap.

Beyond Research#

Outside research, I play the drums and guitar, and I enjoy traveling. I am always happy to connect with people from different backgrounds.
