About#

I am an undergraduate student at Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT) exploring embodied AI and AI for Science. My current interests connect geometric vision with biomedical imaging, especially 3D scene reconstruction, computational pathology, and spatial transcriptomics.

I welcome research discussions and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out if our interests overlap.
